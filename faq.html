FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

Q1: How can I open a new account with Bank Asia?

A: You can open a Savings or Current account conveniently by visiting any of our nearby branches or digitally through the Bank Asia mobile app using e-KYC.

Q2: How do I access Internet Banking or the Smart App?

A: Download the 'Bank Asia Smart App' from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store and complete a quick registration process. Contact our helpline if you need guidance.

Q3: What should I do in case of ATM or card-related emergencies?

A: If your card is lost, stolen, or you notice an unauthorized transaction, please call our 24/7 hotline at 16205 immediately to block your card.
