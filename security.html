Security Guidelines

Your security is our highest priority. To ensure secure online banking, please keep the following in mind:

  • Never share your PIN, OTP (One-Time Password), or account credentials with anyone, including Bank Asia employees.
  • Always verify that you are browsing the official Bank Asia website (www.bankasia-bd.com) to protect against phishing.
  • Report any suspicious activities, emails, or transactions to our customer support immediately.
